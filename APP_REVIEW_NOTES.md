APP REVIEW NOTES

Thank you for reviewing Batik Boutique.

APP OVERVIEW

Batik Boutique is a casual triple-match puzzle game based on traditional Indonesian batik. Players match three identical batik tiles to clear stacked formations, complete levels to earn coins and fabric, run a boutique workshop that produces clothes to fulfill customer requests, and unlock batik motifs in the gallery. Optional skills help complete levels.

The app does not require an account, login, or special credentials. Player progress and settings are stored locally on the device.

HOW TO TEST THE MAIN GAME

1. Launch the app.
2. From the main menu, tap Factory to open the level track.
3. Select an unlocked level.
4. Tap tiles to move them to the tray and make matches of three or more identical batik tiles.
5. Clear the stacked formations before the timer reaches zero.
6. After completing a level, use Next Level to continue or Retry to replay it.

ADDITIONAL FEATURES

Settings:
From the main menu, tap the settings button to switch between English and Bahasa Indonesia and adjust background music and sound-effect volume.

Boutique:
The boutique lets players spend fabric to produce clothes and fulfill customer orders to earn coins. Workshop slots and sewing machines can be upgraded with coins.

Skills:
Skills are available from the level screen and can be purchased with in-game coins. Skills provide effects such as removing tiles, shuffling the board, or adding tray capacity.

Mood:
Failed or abandoned gameplay can reduce the mood meter. Mood recovers over time.

Rewarded ad:
When mood is available to restore, tap Watch Ad and complete two rewarded ads to restore one mood. An internet connection is required, and ad availability may vary during review.

IN-APP PURCHASES

NETWORK AND UNAVAILABLE-SERVICE BEHAVIOR

An internet connection may be required for StoreKit product loading and rewarded ads. If these services are unavailable, the game remains playable without purchases or rewarded ads, and the relevant action displays an unavailable or failed status.
